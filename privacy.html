    • Privacy Policy

    1. About this policy

    RainyDays is a fictional online store created as a school project. This policy explains what information the website uses and why.

    2. Information we store

    The website stores the contents of your shopping cart (which products, sizes and quantities you have chosen) in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your own device and is not sent to any server. You can remove it at any time by emptying your cart or clearing your browser's site data.

    3. Information you enter at checkout

    The checkout page contains a form for name, email, phone number and address. Because this is a simulated checkout, the information you enter is not sent anywhere and is not stored.

    4. Third-party services

    Product data is fetched from a public API provided by Noroff. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and icons are loaded from Font Awesome. Your browser connects to these services when you visit the site, and they may receive technical information such as your IP address.

    5. Cookies

    The website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

    6. Your rights

    Since the website does not collect personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side. In a real store, this section would describe your rights under data protection law, such as GDPR.

    7. Contact

    Questions about this policy can be sent to help@rainydays.com.
