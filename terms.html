    1. About this website

    RainyDays is a fictional online store created as a school project. No real products are sold, no payments are processed, and no orders are shipped. These terms are written as an example of what a real webshop would include.

    2. Products and prices

    Product information, images and prices are fetched from a public demonstration API provided by Noroff. Prices are shown in US dollars and may change without notice. Items marked as on sale show the discounted price at checkout.

    3. Orders

    When you place an order, you receive an order confirmation page. This is a simulation only. No contract of sale is created, and you will not be charged.

    4. Delivery

    The delivery options shown at checkout are examples. No goods are delivered.

    5. Returns and refunds

    Since no real purchases are made, there is nothing to return or refund. In a real store, this section would describe the return period and conditions.

    6. Changes to these terms

    We may update these terms at any time. The latest version is always available on this page.
